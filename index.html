About Me

My story in short

Hey there, I'm Mahlet Amare — a Frontend Developer, UI/UX Designer, Graphics Designer, and Project Manager who enjoys turning ideas into clean, functional digital products. My journey began on campus, where an accidental shift from pre-med to Computer Science turned into a genuine passion for building things. I design user experiences before I design pixels, and I design pixels before I write a single line of code — so by the time I'm coding in HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, I already know exactly why every element belongs where it is. As a Graphics Designer, I bring that same eye for detail into visuals and branding. And as a Project Manager, I make sure ideas don't just look good on paper — they actually ship, on time and as intended.

My goal is simple: create things that look polished and serve a purpose. Whether I'm mapping a user flow, designing a layout, managing a team, or building a full page, I keep exploring new ideas to make something useful. When I'm not working, I like learning new tools and sharpening the details that make a project feel complete.
